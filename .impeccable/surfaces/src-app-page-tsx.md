# Landing page

Scope: the Stringline marketing page (`src/app/page.tsx`). Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: owners and office managers of design-build crews deciding whether to join the spring 2027 pilot list. Action: join the waitlist (email, then the longer application). Belief to land in 10 seconds: "it books me real consults."

Proof and content: no real photos, customers or results exist. Before/after yard images are generated concepts and must be labeled as concepts or examples. Ranges and sample leads are examples. No invented customers, quotes or numbers beyond the MVP scope.

Constraints from the user: the old page was blocky, crammed with information, had no direction, looked AI-made, and its drawings felt cheap. Neutral grounds, colour only as accents. Fewer sections, one idea per screen.

Sections: hero reveal; the 10:47 pm moment in one line; how it works in three steps (answered, booked, shown); pay per shown consult; Aidan; join form; short FAQ; footer.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Show the output, not the software. A homeowner's ordinary yard turns into a priced concept, and that is what fills the calendar. Refuses the field-service split hero with a dashboard screenshot over three feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: A top contractor's project portfolio. White and pale stone grounds, graphite type, large square-cornered photographs that carry all the colour, one string-orange accent on actions and the reveal handle only. Generous space, one dominant image per screen, no cards, no icon rows.

STORY: The visitor watches a yard become a patio with a price range, sees that the homeowner was answered, qualified and booked overnight, learns they pay only for shown consults, and joins the pilot list.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Plain nav (wordmark, three links, Join button). Two-line headline left at display size with one sentence and the primary button. Below it, a full-content-width 16:9 before/after photograph taking about 60% of the viewport, handle centred, Before and Concept labels, an Example range chip on the concept side.

FORM: The Reveal, from the safer re-roll (my second-ranked grounded candidate). seed:0a0ffc1cc540dbbc. Signature interaction: drag to reveal; on load the handle sweeps once from before to concept and settles. Motion grammar: short rises and fades, nothing looping.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
